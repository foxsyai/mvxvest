# dApp review, 12 October 2026

An adversarial review of the first dApp version (commit 6b0b0aa). Every finding came with a test that failed on that code. The tests now live in the dApp next to what they cover, as `review_*.test.ts(x)`, and all of them pass on the fix.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix | Test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Critical | Create: after sending, the app's `mvxvest:settled` event re-rendered the page and gave the signing panel a new `build`. The panel then checked again and offered "Sign in your wallet" for the same vestings, so a second click created every vesting twice. A refresh of the account store during signing did the same while the wallet prompt was open. | TxPanel: a sequence guard on checks, and a lock from the moment the wallet is asked; a re-render never re-arms it. Create: "Check and sign" freezes a snapshot of what was reviewed, and only that snapshot is built and signed. | `pages/tests/review_Create`, `components/tx/tests/review_TxPanel` |
| 2 | High | Create: the form stayed editable while the panel was open, and the panel built from whatever lines still parsed. A line broken after opening was silently dropped from what was signed. | Steps 1 to 4 are disabled while the review is open. If the form still changes before anything is sent, the review closes and asks to check again. | `review_Create` |
| 3 | High | send(): several transactions were POSTed together. If one failed, the whole set was reported as "did not go through" even though the others were already in the mempool, and a retry created them twice. | Transactions are sent one by one, with a retry on 429, 5xx and network errors. A partial send is reported as such: the sent ones are tracked and the nonce is reset. Create offers "Sign the remaining N" for the rest only, from the right nonce. | `chain/tests/review_send` |
| 4 | High | Gas for a guarded account could reach 600,050,000, above the network maximum. A simulated cost above 600M was capped and shown as "would go through". | The cap includes the guardian's extra gas. A cost above the cap is a problem ("send fewer vestings at once"). | `review_send` |
| 5 | High | Amounts with a decimal comma: "0,05" was read as 5 and "1,5" as 15. In a semicolon CSV, "1.500,75" was read as 1.50075. | A comma counts as a thousands separator only where it cannot be a decimal comma ("1,234.5", "1,234,567"). Anything else is refused with a reason, never guessed. Percentages accept either mark, with at most two decimals and no rounding. | `vesting/tests/review_amounts` |
| 6 | High | Batches of vestings with many tranches: gas was assumed to be 0.42M per tranche, but devnet measured 132M for one 120-tranche create. That put 8 per batch, about 1,000M gas. | Measured fit 0.3624M·n + 0.005216M·n², plus 15%. 120 tranches now gives 3 per transaction. | `vesting/tests/review_batches` |
| 7 | Medium | CSV: line 1 was taken for a header whenever it did not start with "erd1", so an upper-case address or a typo on the first row vanished without a word. | A header is skipped only if its cells are neither an address nor a number. Rows typed one by one are never taken for a header. | `vesting/tests/review_csv` |
| 8 | Medium | CSV export: a label starting with `= + - @` became a spreadsheet formula, and a carriage return was not quoted. | Formula-like text gets a leading apostrophe; CR is quoted. | `review_csv` |
| 9 | Medium | A cleared date or a share like "10%" (NaN) reached `BigInt(NaN)` in render, which blanked the whole app. | The plan, the schedule check and the release list treat non-finite values as a problem, never a crash. | `vesting/tests/review_builder` |
| 10 | Medium | "Every N months, on the same day" was anchored in UTC. East of UTC, a start late in the month gave releases on the 1st instead of the last day of the month. | Months are anchored in the reader's own calendar (the zone the form is typed in). Tests run in Europe/Bucharest. | `review_builder`, `builder.test` |
| 11 | Medium | Vesting history: the API's topic filter matches any topic, so for small ids, key transfers (quantity 01) filled the 1,000-event window and vesting #1 showed no "Created". | One query per endpoint (create, createBatch, claim, cancel), each read to its end. | `chain/tests/review_history` |
| 12 | Medium | The vesting page said "No vesting with this number" whenever a read failed, including on 429. | Only the contract's "unknown vesting" means that. Any other failure says the network did not answer and offers "Try again". A key the API has not indexed yet reads "not listed yet", not burned. | `pages/tests/review_Vesting` |
| 13 | Medium | Token decimals were guessed (18) until the API answered, and for ever if it failed: 1,000,000 USDC showed as 0.000001. Moving between vestings showed the previous token's decimals. | No figure is shown until the decimals are known, and a failed read offers a retry. `useAsync` no longer keeps data across different inputs. | `hooks/tests/review_useToken` |
| 14 | Low | The chart drew at most 2,000 releases and then a flat line, so 10 years of daily releases looked 55% vested at the end. | Past 400 releases the curve is sampled to its end. | `components/vesting/tests/review_Chart` |
| 15 | Low | Mine and Created spun for ever on a failed read. The Token page computed "vested" from the newest 2,000 vestings without saying so; Created listed the newest 500 without saying so. | An error with "Try again" on every list page; a note wherever the list is partial. | — |
| 16 | Low | Labels are arbitrary text on chain and could reorder the surrounding text (bidi). | Labels are isolated with `<bdi>`. | — |
| 17 | Info | The Guide said the owner "has no way to move locked tokens" next to "the contract can be upgraded". | The copy says the promises hold for the code as it is, and that an upgrade by the multisig replaces it. | — |
| 18 | Info | deploy.sh built without checking the lockfile or the tests. | `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` and the full test suite run before every build. | — |

## Second review, of the fixes (commit 3d35c88)

Another adversarial pass checked every fix above. Each finding came with a failing test, kept as `review2_*.test.ts(x)`. All of them pass on the fix. It confirmed fixes 1–4 and 10–18, plus the cases 5, 7, 8 and 9 were written for. It also found these gaps:

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix | Test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | High | A send that timed out in the browser (sdk-dapp gives up after 4 s; a round is 0.6 s) had often already been executed. The retry was then refused for its nonce, and the page reported "did not go through" (first transaction) or "not sent" (a later one). Its "Check again" or "Sign the remaining" then created those vestings a second time. | After a try that may have arrived (no answer, or a 5xx), a nonce refusal means it was executed and counts as sent. If no try was confirmed, it is reported as unconfirmed, never as lost, and nothing after it is sent. The panel follows it, and only calls it "not found" if the API has not seen it after 2 minutes. | `chain/tests/review2_send` |
| R2 | High | After a partial send, "Not now" on the remaining panel dropped the review, unlocked the form and let every vesting be signed again. | The review records a hash for each transaction. Once anything is sent it stays, and the form stays locked, until "Start a new creation". Closing a panel only removes that panel. | `pages/tests/review2_Create` |
| R3 | Medium | After a wallet account switch, the remaining transactions were signed by the new account with the old account's nonce. A review opened by one account could also be signed by another. | Only the account that opened the review signs any of it. Before anything is sent, a switch closes the review. After, the page asks for that account to be connected again. | `review2_Create` |
| R4 | Medium | The panel for the remaining transactions showed the whole review's amount, not what it signs. | Each signing shows its own vestings, total and label. | `review2_Create` |
| R5 | Medium | "Sign the remaining" unmounted the first panel before it had followed its transactions to the end, so their created vestings never showed. | Every panel that sent something stays on the page and keeps following its transactions. A transaction that fails on chain, or is never found, is offered again; a successful one never is. | `review2_Create` |
| R6 | Low | A huge "every N days or months" passed the checks, and drawing the preview crashed the app. | Plans longer than the contract's 30 years are refused before anything is built. Date formatting never throws. | `review2_Create` |
| R7 | Low | In a semicolon-separated (European) file, "10.000" was read as 10. | In such files, a point followed by exactly three digits is refused with an explanation. | `vesting/tests/review2_amounts` |
| R8 | Low | A placeholder line right after the header was skipped too. | Only the first non-empty line can be a header. | `vesting/tests/review2_csv` |
| R9 | Low | In a semicolon-locale spreadsheet, a label holding `;=...` became a formula cell in the export. | Cells with any separator are quoted, and a formula start after an inner `;` or tab gets an apostrophe. | `review2_csv` |

From the review's notes (no test):

- **Auto-close while signing:** the review no longer closes while the wallet is asked.
- **Dead Close button:** the no-op "Close" is gone.
- **Builds:** each signing has its own build.
- **Batches:** they are memoised.
- **Leaving the page:** the browser warns before you leave with transactions half done. Navigating inside the app still loses that record.
- **Vesting numbers:** a number beyond 2^53 reads "No vesting".
