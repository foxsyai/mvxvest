Claiming your tokens at mvxvest.io

A short guide for the beneficiaries of a vesting

Someone locked tokens for you in a vesting. They unlock over time. You collect what has unlocked whenever you like.

Your wallet holds the vesting's claim key: an NFT called Vesting #… claim key, like the one on the left. Whoever holds it can claim.

Your part takes about a minute. It is the same every time.

1

Open mvxvest.io and connect your wallet

  • Go to mvxvest.io and press Connect. Check the address in your browser: only mvxvest.io.
  • Choose how you sign: xPortal (scan the code with your phone), the MultiversX browser extension, a Ledger or the web wallet.
  • Connect the wallet that holds the claim key. Connecting only shows the site your address; it cannot move anything.
2

Open My vestings

  • Press My vestings at the top. You see every vesting whose claim key is in your wallet.
  • Each card shows what has vested so far, what you already claimed and what you can claim now.
3

Claim

  • Press Claim on one card. Claim all collects from every vesting at once.
  • The page first checks with the network and shows the most the network fee can be. Then press Sign in your wallet and approve in your wallet. A Ledger asks you to confirm each transaction on the device.
  • Before you approve, check that your wallet shows the vesting contract as the receiver: erd1qqqqqqqqqqqqqpgqlzpdsymdk7zxekm9pvtpxja4gfx6trwluc2qgmllsu. A copy of the site elsewhere can look the same, but it cannot fake that address.
  • Claiming costs only that small network fee, paid in EGLD. Keep a little EGLD in your wallet: around 0.05 is plenty.
4

Done

  • The page tells you what reached your wallet, token by token. The tokens are in your wallet now.
  • The claim key comes back to you for the next time. Once everything is claimed it is burned: that vesting is finished.
Good to know
  • You can claim as often as you like. Waiting loses nothing.
  • Some vestings grow every second; others unlock on set dates. Each vesting's page shows the next release.
  • If the creator can cancel your vesting, what has vested by then stays yours.
  • Sending the claim key to another wallet gives that wallet the right to claim. Only do it on purpose.
  • Nobody from mvxvest will ever ask for your secret words or your keys.
mvxvest is open source: github.com/foxsyai/mvxvest. It never holds your keys: every transaction is signed in your own wallet. The full guide, for creators too: mvxvest.io/guide.
